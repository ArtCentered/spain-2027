# Photos for the Sol y Norte app

Each day has its own folder: `photos/trip/day-NN/`. Two kinds of file:

- `header.jpg` — the day's hero banner (landscape, ~1200×600 works well). Also used as the thumbnail in the day list.
- `<slug>.jpg` — appears in that event's photo gallery on the day's timeline (any size; landscape best). If it's missing the gallery simply doesn't show.

Add more slugs to any event in `index.html` (`photos:[cap(N,'slug','caption')]`) — or just tell me and I'll wire them.
